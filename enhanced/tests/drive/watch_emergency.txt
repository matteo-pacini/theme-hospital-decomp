# Level 1: the panel's emergency watch. An emergency starts (the `watch`
# hook) and the helicopter brings three patients (things 24, 30, 31). A right
# click on the watch (coords[0x84], watch[13]'s size: process_right_button
# case 0x27) picks the next emergency patient (find_next_emergency_person)
# and opens their window with EV_OPEN_THING_WINDOW; each further click moves
# on to the next one, wrapping round to the first. A left click on the watch
# then centres the view on the picked patient (screenshot).
wait 60
watch emergency
turns 400
wait 10
expect emergency_patient=0 epidemic_patient=0
expect query window: patient false
click 20 360 right       # the watch
wait 10
expect emergency_patient=24
expect query window: patient true (thing 24,
screenshot watch_emergency_first
click 20 360 right
wait 10
expect emergency_patient=30
expect query window: patient true (thing 30,
click 20 360 right
wait 10
expect emergency_patient=31
expect query window: patient true (thing 31,
click 20 360 right
wait 10
expect emergency_patient=24
expect query window: patient true (thing 24,
click 20 360             # left: centre on the picked patient
wait 10
screenshot watch_emergency_centred
quit
